On **Thursday, November 14, 2025**, the **Eko Boys to Men Association (EB2M)** will host a **Mentoring Workshop for Secondary Schools** on Lagos Island, themed **“Career Development and Skills Acquisition"**.

### Build Confidence, Purpose, and Practical Skills

This workshop will bring together professionals, educators, and mentors — many of them distinguished old boys of Eko Boys High School — to share insights, life lessons, and practical knowledge with students.
The goal is to equip these young men and women with a sense of direction and the confidence to pursue meaningful careers, whether through formal education or skill-based entrepreneurship.

Through interactive sessions, career talks, and hands-on learning, participants will gain exposure to different career paths and the evolving world of work, preparing them for the challenges and opportunities ahead.

---

### 🤝 A Lifelong Commitment to Giving Back

The Eko Boys to Men Association has consistently demonstrated that mentorship is one of the most powerful ways to give back to the community. By investing time and knowledge into the next generation, members continue to uphold the values of leadership, responsibility, and excellence instilled during their school days.

This year’s workshop underscores that belief — that *every student, regardless of background, deserves access to guidance that helps them dream bigger and achieve more*.
