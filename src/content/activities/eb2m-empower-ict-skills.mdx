The **Eko Boys to Men Association** has advised students to make good use of technology to navigate their various fields in the future. **Information Communication and Technology (ICT)** skills are needed to succeed in a competitive, tech-driven world.

During an engagement with school kids, the association emphasised that digital literacy is no longer optional — it is a core foundation for every modern career, from engineering and medicine to business and the creative arts.

### Preparing Students for a Tech-Driven World

Members of the association encouraged the students to embrace technology early, build practical ICT skills, and remain curious about the tools shaping the world around them. By developing these skills while still in school, the students position themselves to compete and thrive in whatever field they choose.
