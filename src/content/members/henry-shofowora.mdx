Henry O. Shofowora, ACIPM, CMI, MNIM, TFS, is a distinguished management consultant, international trade finance professional, corporate trainer, and business executive with over two decades of experience in banking, leadership, and organisational development.

He is the Managing Consultant/Chief Executive Officer of MSNL Consulting & MSNL Academy, a firm specialising in executive education, management consulting, leadership development, international trade, banking operations, and professional capacity building.

Henry is an Associate Member of the Chartered Institute of Personnel Management (CIPM) of Nigeria, an Associate Member of the Chartered Management Institute (CMI), United Kingdom, and a Full Member of the Nigerian Institute of Management (Chartered). Since 2009, he has served as a member of the training faculty of the International Chamber of Commerce (ICC) Nigeria Banking Commission, facilitating professional development programmes for banking and corporate professionals.

A passionate advocate for lifelong learning and community development, Henry serves as a Trustee of Eko Boys to Men Association (EB2M), where he contributes his expertise to strengthening governance, leadership, mentorship, and initiatives that promote unity, professional excellence, and sustainable community impact among members.
