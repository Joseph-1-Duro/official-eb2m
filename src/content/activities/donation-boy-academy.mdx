Members of Eko Boys to Men Association on Thursday, 6th October, 2022 donated Borehole and Water treatment plant to Boys Junior Academy, Simpson street, Lagos Island as a way of giving back to the society
The gesture, according to the Chairman of the Association, Mr. Effiong Okon, is to provide access to quality safe and drinkable water for the students.

Before the official hand over of the donated Borehole and Water treatment plant to the school Management, the Association had earlier held a lecture & mentorship session for students from different schools on Lagos Island at Dolphin Senior High school , Lagos Island.
