🎉 CONGRATULATIONS TO MR. EFFIONG OKON 🎉

On behalf of the entire members of Eko Boys to Men Association, we heartily congratulate our distinguished President, Mr. Effiong Okon, on his appointment as the Chief Executive Officer of Seplat Energy Plc.

This prestigious appointment is a testament to your exceptional leadership, professional excellence, and unwavering commitment to the growth of Nigeria’s energy sector. We are proud to witness this remarkable milestone in your illustrious career.

As you assume this important responsibility, we wish you tremendous success, wisdom, and strength to lead Seplat Energy Plc to even greater heights. May your tenure be highly productive, impactful, and filled with outstanding achievements that will further strengthen the organization’s position as a leading indigenous energy company.

Your success continues to inspire us all, and we are confident that your leadership will leave a lasting legacy of innovation, growth, and excellence.

Congratulations once again, Mr. Effiong Okon. We wish you a successful and rewarding tenure.

Signed:

- Ade Odubade
- BOT Member
- Eko Boys to Men Association
